# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

University students planning target grades. Confirmed by the user.

## Product Purpose

UniGrade lets students record course assessments, see calculated grades and GPA, and plan toward desired results. The user's stated priority is reaching target grades quickly in a redesigned educational interface that feels fast.

## Operating Context

Students review current term performance, enter scores as assessments arrive, inspect a course, plan target grades, and check deadlines. They may use the site on desktop or mobile.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing capabilities in the source: course scores, term and cumulative GPA, grade targets, planner, analytics, local storage, optional Google sync, export and sharing.
- Keep existing calculations and stored data compatible during the visual redesign.
- Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript project; no framework build step.
- Confirmed priority: make target-grade planning the quickest primary flow.

## Brand Commitments

- The name UniGrade appears throughout the interface.
- The user previously requested an app-wide logo replacement. The visual identity may change again during this redesign; preserve functioning icon coverage across browser and install surfaces.
- Thai is the primary interface language in the existing product.

## Evidence on Hand

- Working implementation: `index.html` and `redesign.css`.
- Local icon assets: `app-icon.svg` and PNG variants.
- No verified university partnership or institutional branding claim is available.

## Product Principles

- Make progress toward a chosen grade target easy to understand at a glance.
- Put the next useful action close to the information that motivates it.
- Preserve entered work and make calculation states legible.
- Keep the interface usable on small screens and responsive to input.
